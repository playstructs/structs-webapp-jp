# 用語集: Japanese game vocabulary

One word per idea, everywhere. Struct-type data is in `runtime/terms.json` and
agrees with this page.

## Voice

- Players are strategy gamers, not crypto users. No ブロックチェーン, ウォレット,
  トークン, トランザクション, ガス, 署名 where a game word does the job.
- Help text and system messages: です・ます. Intro narration: である調.
- HR Bot and corporate callers: stiff, over-polite 敬語 (…でございます, 社員殿).
- Buttons, tabs, headers: short nouns or dictionary-form verbs (確認, 配備).
- Player: あなた only where it can't be dropped. Names take さん in greetings.
- `? ! : ( )` stay half-width (they come from webapp's own pixel font); `、。「」`
  are Japanese. Ellipsis is `...`.
- Numbers, units and ids stay as they are: `W`, `kW`, `g`, `PID`, `#`, `1-42`.
